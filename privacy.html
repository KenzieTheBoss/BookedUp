This policy explains what information BookedUp collects when you contact us, how that information is used, and how it is protected. It applies to the text messaging service operated by BookedUp at (909) 506-0028.

How You Reach Us

BookedUp only exchanges text messages with people who contact BookedUp first. There are three ways that happens:

  • You submit the service request form on our website and check the SMS consent box.
  • You text the keyword BOOK to (909) 506-0028.
  • You call (909) 506-0028. If we are unable to answer, we reply by text to that number to help you with your request.

We never send a message to a number that has not contacted us first. We do not purchase, rent, or upload phone number lists.

Information We Collect

  • Your phone number
  • The content of text messages you send and receive
  • Information you provide during a conversation, such as your name, service address, and a description of what you need
  • Appointment details, including the date and time you schedule
  • The date and time of your calls and messages

How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to your request and answer your questions
  • Schedule, confirm, and remind you about appointments
  • Maintain records required for messaging compliance, including opt-in and opt-out records
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell your personal information or your SMS opt-in data to anyone, for any purpose.

Who We Share Information With

BookedUp uses service providers to operate the service: a telecommunications provider for message delivery, a calendar provider for appointment scheduling, an AI provider for generating replies, and a cloud hosting provider. Each processes information only to perform its function and is not permitted to use it for its own marketing.

Where BookedUp operates a text line on behalf of a client business, the information you provide is shared with that business so it can perform the service you requested. You are told which business you are messaging in the first message you receive.

BookedUp may disclose information where required by law.

Message Frequency and Opt-Out

Message frequency varies based on your conversation. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply START to opt back in, or HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

BookedUp retains conversation records and appointment information for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and compliance obligations. Opt-out and opt-in records are retained permanently, because messaging law requires proof that opt-out requests are honored. Those records contain only a phone number, a timestamp, and the action taken.

Your Choices

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing kenzie.bookedup@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Opt-out records are the one exception and are retained as described above.

California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA to know what personal information is collected about you, to request its deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Send any such request to the email address above.

Security

BookedUp uses encrypted connections and restricts access to stored data. No system is perfectly secure, and BookedUp does not guarantee that it is.

Children's Privacy

BookedUp's services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
