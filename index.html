Home service appointments, scheduled by text.

BookedUp is an appointment scheduling service for home services businesses — plumbing, HVAC, and similar trades — serving Pomona and the San Gabriel Valley, California. Tell us what you need, and we'll text you available appointment times and confirm your booking.

How it works

  1. You request service using the form below, text us, or call and we miss you.
  2. We text you to confirm what you need and where you're located.
  3. We send you real available appointment times.
  4. You pick one, and we confirm your booking by text.

Texts are sent only to people who contact us first. We never send marketing or promotional messages.
